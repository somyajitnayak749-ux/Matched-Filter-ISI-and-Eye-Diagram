Matched filter,ISI and Eye Diagram
**Matched Filter** maximizes Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) at the sampling instant to optimize detection in noise.

**Inter-Symbol Interference (ISI)** occurs when adjacent pulses overlap due to channel distortion, causing detection errors.

**Eye Diagram** superimposes signal traces on an oscilloscope; its opening reveals noise margin, timing jitter, and ISI severity.
